# Vendor Questionnaire

## Commercial market-data provider

Business description:

> We operate a browser-based simulated trading training and weekly performance-competition platform. Users trade fictitious balances only. We display real-time market prices in our own trading terminal and simulate all order execution internally. No customer capital is routed to financial markets.

Please confirm:

1. Do your commercial terms allow this business model?
2. May we display your real-time data to paying end users inside our simulator?
3. May our backend consume one/few upstream streams and redistribute normalized prices to our authenticated users?
4. What forex pairs are available?
5. Is XAU/USD available?
6. Which indices/index-CFD reference prices are available?
7. Do you provide bid and ask, or only mid/last?
8. What is the typical update frequency/latency?
9. Is streaming available by WebSocket?
10. Are historical candles available for 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h and 1d?
11. Do you provide symbol specifications/market hours?
12. Can we serve 10,000 active weekly users / ~2,000 concurrent users under one commercial agreement?
13. Are there per-user/display fees?
14. Are there exchange/vendor pass-through fees?
15. Is attribution required?
16. Can we cache intraday data? For how long?
17. Can we store historical quote/order evidence for dispute/audit purposes?
18. What happens during market-data corrections or outages?
19. What production SLA/support applies?
20. Quote the lowest-cost plan that explicitly permits this usage.

Get usage/display/redistribution rights in writing before production.
